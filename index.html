Teil 01
Die Branchen von Schrade — was ich herausgefunden habe

Schrade beliefert laut Website funf Hauptbereiche. Ich habe fur jeden Bereich die aktuellen Wachstumszahlen recherchiert. Was mich ehrlich gesagt am meisten uberrascht hat war der Robotik-Markt — die Zahlen sind deutlich hoher als ich erwartet hatte.

Robotics & Automation
Der starkste Wachstumsmarkt
+16.6%
CAGR 2024 bis 2034 — Globaler Markt: $47.8B heute, $211B bis 2034
This was the number that really stood out to me. Every robot needs cables in its joints and control systems and the demand for flexible high cycle harnesses is growing fast especially with the new humanoid robot projects coming from different companies. I think this could become Schrade's most important segment in the next few years.
Quelle: GM Insights 2025, VDMA Robotics 2026
Medizintechnik
Regulierung als Chance
+8.2%
CAGR 2024 bis 2030 — Deutschland ist der groste EU Markt
What I found interesting here is the MDR 2024 regulation. It is forcing medical companies to work with fewer but more certified suppliers. That could actually be good for Schrade if they are already certified — because smaller competitors might struggle with the certification costs and drop out of this market.
Quelle: ZVEI Medizintechnik 2025, Statista
Aerospace & Sonderfahrzeuge
Airbus Auftragsruckstand als Signal
+7.5%
CAGR 2025 bis 2034 — Airbus Auftragsbestand uber 8700 Flugzeuge
I did not realise how much cable goes into a single aircraft — apparently between 200 and 600 km per plane. With the Airbus backlog at over 8700 units that is a lot of cable assembly work for years to come. The defence vehicles segment also looks interesting given what is happening with EU budgets right now.
Quelle: GM Insights Machine Tools 2025
Energietechnik
Energiewende treibt Investitionen
+5.5%
CAGR 2025 bis 2030 — Netzerneuerung und erneuerbare Energien
The ZVEI published their forecast in January 2026 and they expect the German electro and digital industry to grow again by about 2 percent in 2026 after three years of stagnation. That is not huge growth but it is a clear signal that investment is coming back. The grid modernisation projects need a lot of cable infrastructure.
Quelle: ZVEI Jahresauftakt Januar 2026
Landmaschinen
Elektrifizierung der Landwirtschaft
+4.8%
CAGR 2025 bis 2030 — Elektrische Traktoren und Prazisionslandwirtschaft
This one surprised me a bit. Electric tractors and precision farming sensors are creating much more complex cable requirements than traditional machines. The VDMA reported order intake in agricultural machinery was up 17 percent in December 2025 which is a positive sign for the whole supply chain.

Teil 03
Was mir aufgefallen ist

A few things that came to mind while I was reading about Schrade and its markets. I am not saying these are problems — I just noticed them and thought they might be worth thinking about from a data side.

GEDANKE 01
Robotik wachst am schnellsten — aber ist es auch das grosste Segment?
With 16.6 percent CAGR Robotics is clearly growing the fastest but I could not find any public information about how much of Schrade's current revenue comes from this segment. If it is still small it might be worth tracking it more closely — because fast growth in a small segment can easily be missed if you only look at total revenue numbers.
GEDANKE 02
Berichte an Infinite Electronics — einheitliche KPIs waren hilfreich
I noticed that Schrade is part of Infinite Electronics in the US. In my experience with data work, when a subsidiary reports to a parent company the biggest challenge is usually that the KPI definitions are slightly different on each side. Having one consistent dashboard that both sides understand would probably save a lot of time every month.
GEDANKE 03
MDR 2024 konnte eine Chance fur Schrade sein
The new EU medical device regulation is making it harder for smaller suppliers to stay certified. If Schrade already meets the certification requirements that could become a real advantage — because medical OEMs are being pushed to reduce their supplier base. I think this would be interesting to track as a KPI: how many new medical customers are coming from competitors who dropped out.
GEDANKE 04
Liefertreue als kritischer KPI im Aerospace Bereich
In aerospace even a small delay in a cable assembly can stop an entire production line. That makes on time delivery probably the most important KPI for this segment. I would think about tracking it separately per customer segment rather than as one overall number — because a 95 percent average can hide a 70 percent rate in the most critical segment.
Teil 04
Ein paar Ideen — falls sie nutzlich sind
Was ich beitragen konnte — ehrlich gesagt
I want to be upfront — I do not know Schrade's internal systems yet and I am still learning. But based on what I found this weekend, here are a few things I think I could help with if I got the chance.

The reporting to Infinite Electronics sounds like something that could be made more automatic. Right now I imagine someone is probably exporting data from one system and reformating it manually every month. I have done this kind of ETL work in Python before — connecting to a database, cleaning the data, and outputting a structured report. It is not the most exciting work but it saves a lot of time.

The other thing that caught my attention is the five different industry segments. Each one has very different growth rates and risk profiles. A simple dashboard that shows how each segment is performing relative to its market CAGR would make it much easier to see early if one segment is falling behind — or growing faster than expected.
